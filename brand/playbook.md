# Playbook v1

Status: initial rules from the build prompt (no performance data yet). Changes need owner approval while `LEARNING_MODE=propose`. Every change gets a changelog entry below and can be rolled back with git.

## Hooks (spec 7.2)

- 10 hooks per chosen idea from `brand/hooks.json`; score 1–10 on: scroll-stop in < 1 s, ≤ 7 on-screen words spoken in < 2 s, curiosity gap, specificity (number/name/place/time), truthfulness, first-frame visual match.
- Keep the best as primary plus 2 alternates for trial-reel A/B tests.

## Visual hook

- Motion in the very first frame; zoom punch 0–0.4 s; bold hook text inside the safe zone; sound hit at 0 s; first spoken word within 0.3 s; no logo, no intro.

## Retention

- Pattern interrupt (cut, zoom, colour flash or new visual) every 2–3 s.
- Open loop in the first 3 s, paid off at the end.
- Thin progress bar. Last line loops back into the first.

## Structure

- Hook (0–2 s) → Setup → 2–4 Escalation → Payoff → Loop/CTA. 20–40 s, about 60–110 words.
- On-screen text ≤ 6 words per beat.

## Connection

- One specific question for comments per reel.
- Turn performing topics into series ("Part 1/3"); take "Part 2" topics from comment requests.

## Content safety

- Hypotheticals framed as "What if…". Facts only with claim-level evidence (SUPPORTED) or rewritten as hypothetical.
- No real people's likeness, logos, copyrighted characters, medical/financial/legal advice, fake news, or content aimed at children.

## Learning guardrails

- No strategy change without ≥ 8 reels per compared group and ≥ 7 days of data.
- Default scoring weights: watch time 35%, shares 25%, saves 20%, comments 10%, skip rate 10%.
